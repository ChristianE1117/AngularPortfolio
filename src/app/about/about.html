About Me

Christian Ebert

Student studing Software Development

Christian is a student at North Central Technical College working to become a full-time software developer.

Before enrolling in NTC, Christian graduated high school from Colfax High School in Colfax Wisconsin. Here he gained a liking to programming during his junior year. That year he was asked to take a distance learning college class to help fill the student requirement to have the class. During this class he learned the basics of C# programming and what it is capeable of. Then in his senior year he went and took another 12 credits towards his associates degree.

While in high school Christian also participated in multiple extra-cirriculars. During the fall he played Football. Then in the winter he participated in powerlifting and bowling. And during the spring season he was on the Golf team. During his time on the bowling team, he lead the team to the state championships in all four years of high school and had two top 10 finishes. Christian was also the Colfax FFA Chapter President for his Junior and Senior years. Within the FFA he learned many life skills that are applicable to any future carrer. He learned leadership, delegation, public speaking and time management.
